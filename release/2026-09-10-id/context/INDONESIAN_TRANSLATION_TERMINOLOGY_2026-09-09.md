## 8. Style rules

1. Preserve the source claim strength: “suggests”, “indicates”, “may”, and similar qualifiers must not be strengthened into certainty.
2. Do not add explanations, examples, citations, or literature not present in the English baseline.
3. Do not silently correct source content during translation; any substantive issue must be handled as a separate controlled correction.
4. Preserve technical acronyms and identifiers.
5. Preserve original paper titles and bibliography verbatim.
6. Prefer Indonesian academic prose that reads naturally, but keep technical terms in English where translation would reduce precision.
7. Use one preferred translation consistently throughout the manuscript.
8. Numeric punctuation follows the source manuscript for analytical values; do not localize numbers in a way that changes machine/audit comparison.
9. “AI-for-DevOps” remains the scope label and must not be conflated with MLOps or DevOps-for-AI.
10. “AIOps” remains an application paradigm/context, not an algorithm category.

## 9. Translation-change control

If a term not covered here creates ambiguity during translation, add it to this terminology file before using multiple competing translations in the manuscript. Any terminology change that could alter analytical meaning requires explicit review before propagation.
