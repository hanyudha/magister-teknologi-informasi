# Appendix C — Quality Assessment

Quality Assessment (QA) was conducted for all primary studies retained after full-text eligibility screening. The purpose of QA was to ensure that each included study met a minimum level of methodological clarity and evidence adequacy before contributing to the synthesis.

## C.1 Quality Assessment Questions

Five quality questions were applied:

| ID | Quality Question |
|---|---|
| QA1 | Is the research objective clearly described? |
| QA2 | Are the AI application and DevOps context clearly described? |
| QA3 | Is the research/evaluation method adequately described? |
| QA4 | Are the reported results supported by evidence or data? |
| QA5 | Are study limitations, threats, implications, or equivalent constraints discussed? |

Each question was scored using:

```text
Yes     = 1.0
Partial = 0.5
No      = 0.0
```

The maximum QA score was **5.0**, and the inclusion threshold was:

```text
QA Score >= 3.0 / 5
```

## C.2 Quality Assessment Outcome

All **60 included primary studies** were assessed.

```text
QA assessed = 60
QA PASS     = 60
QA FAIL     = 0
```

Thus, no study was removed from the final corpus at the QA stage.

A PASS result was treated as a minimum quality threshold and did not imply that all studies contributed evidence of equal strength. Evidence strength was assessed separately through categories such as Production, Industrial Empirical, Controlled Experimental, Benchmark, Simulation, and Conceptual.

## C.3 Interpretation of QA

QA was used as a methodological eligibility safeguard rather than as a direct measure of production validity or practical impact.

For example, a study could satisfy all minimum reporting and methodological requirements while still being based on:

- a benchmark dataset;
- a simulation;
- a controlled experimental setting; or
- a conceptual or applied demonstration.

Accordingly, synthesis interpretation considered QA status together with the study's industrial context, evidence type, and evidence strength.

## C.4 Reproducibility Limitation

The final repository preserves the QA instrument, scoring rule, PASS threshold, and final status of all 60 studies. The canonical extraction workbook records each study as:

```text
PASS — exact score unrecovered
```

Therefore, the exact historical numeric QA score and individual QA1–QA5 decisions for each primary study cannot currently be reconstructed from the preserved final artifacts.

This appendix deliberately does not estimate or recreate those numeric scores.

The verifiable QA conclusion is limited to:

```text
60/60 studies assessed
60 PASS
0 FAIL
threshold >= 3.0 / 5
```

If the original batch-level QA scoring artifacts are recovered in the future, they should be reconciled against the 60-study corpus before any paper-level numeric QA table is added to the supplementary dataset.
